用途：买瓜名场面火柴人二创试片，单镜头，12 秒，16:9。直接保留原剧情的核心节奏：顾客来到瓜摊询价，追问“这瓜保熟吗？”，摊主自信保证，试切后双方看到不够红的瓜瓤，顾客质疑、摊主恼羞，最后夸张地面对面僵住。只取剧情，不导入原片画面、原声或水印。

正向提示词（参考本地 H3 T2V 的单镜头动作描述，也适用于 AnimateDiff 的 positive conditioning）：
Warm white paper background, hand-drawn 2D stick-figure comedy, thick expressive black ink outlines, a lively small watermelon stall with striped awning, handwritten price sign, crates and melon leaves. Two clearly different adult stick figures: the seller wears a green cap and striped apron; the customer wears a short red scarf. Exaggerated elastic faces with moving eyebrows, narrowed suspicious eyes, wide shocked eyes, asymmetric smirks, sweat drop and clenched mouth. Locked camera, readable left-to-right staging, seller on left, customer on right, one watermelon in the center. The customer jogs in and asks the price, points at the melon, leans forward to ask if it is ripe. The seller nods too confidently. The seller cuts a small wedge for inspection; the pale pink flesh makes both faces snap into surprise. The customer points at the wedge, the seller bristles, and both lean into a silent comedic standoff. Secondary motion: scarf and apron follow-through, swinging price sign, bouncing melon leaf, brief squash-and-stretch on the reveal. Mostly drawings held for two output frames at 24 fps, a few single-frame impact drawings and longer held reaction poses. Clear anticipation, impact, reaction and pause. Small green and pink accents, otherwise ink-and-paper. 1280x720 final composition, no camera movement.

负向提示词（参考本地 AnimateDiff 的 negative conditioning）：
No photorealism, no changing character identities, no extra people or limbs, no object morphing, no unreadable hands, no moving camera, no scene cuts, no dense subtitles, no logos or watermarks, no original film frames or audio, no realistic injury or gore, no melon running away.

制作说明：本地 H3 T2V 示例默认 608×352、73 帧、24 fps，不足以在一次生成中稳定覆盖 12 秒多段剧情；因此本次先用独立可复现的分镜绘制渲染 720p 试片，不把该文本误称为已由 H3 执行。后续若用 H3，应按“询价/保熟/试切/反应”拆成多个短镜头，逐镜头人工确认角色与道具连续性。
